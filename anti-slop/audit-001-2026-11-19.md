# antislop audit 001, 2026-11-19

Scope: the redesign pass on branch `preview` (hero and logo form, closing band, footer logo, agent flow, step words, hours band, agent rail, header, card type scale, copy trims, mobile sweep). Checked against the production build on localhost at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1180, 1280, 1440 and 1920px.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Lock = LOW. Nothing below has been changed yet. Reply with the numbers to fix.

## HIGH

1. **The floating "Contact us" button covers body text on phones** (R-03). At 390px it sits over the ends of the lines in the Talent Marketplace section and on other long paragraphs near the bottom of the screen. Fix: reserve space for it (bottom padding on the last sections under 640px) or shrink it to an icon that clears the text column.
2. **The disappearing header was not reproduced** (R-35). Scrolling the home page at 8 widths and navigating across 8 routes never hid or covered the header; no element paints over it. Two related bugs were found and fixed (new pages landing part-way down after navigation; the header CTA vanishing between 1024 and 1279px). If the header still disappears, the browser, device and page where it happens are needed to reproduce it.
3. **Smooth arrow scrolling on the agent rail could not be confirmed in the test browser** (R-35). The test harness throttles smooth element scrolling (a plain test scroller moved 71 of 500px in one second). The arrow logic was verified on the instant path: one card per click, to the end and back, with the end states correct. One click-through in a normal browser window is still needed.
4. **Some text links are smaller than 44px tall on touch** (R-03). Text-button CTAs, breadcrumbs and filter chips were fixed. Still small: inline agent and POC links in lists (24px), "Watch POC" links on industry pages (20px), and the email and phone links on /contact (17px, also under the 24px WCAG minimum). Fix: the same invisible hit-area extension used for text buttons.

## MEDIUM

5. **Status chip and breadcrumb colours bypass the brand tokens** (R-29, BRANDING.md token rule). `StatusChip` uses Tailwind emerald, sky and slate; the breadcrumb uses slate-400 and slate-200. Fix: add status tokens to `app/globals.css` and use `--txm-text-on-dark` for the breadcrumb.
6. **Two trust items repeat their statement in the detail line** (R-36). "Workato Silver Partner" and "Claude Partner Network member" say the same thing twice. Not changed, because `content/trust.ts` requires security-owner sign-off.
7. **The AI Governance description is still 25 words** (R-36, card trim). Left as written so "where they apply" is not lost. Logged in CONTENT-TODO.md for counsel or the security owner.

## LOW

8. **The LATAM row in the hours band has no local clock**, while the US and India rows do (R-14). Probably because LATAM spans several time zones; a label such as "UTC-3 to UTC-6" would keep the rows consistent.
9. **Closing band brightness is a judgement call** (R-13). It now runs at 0.55 against the hero's 0.8 and sits clear of the headline, but on desktop it is still vivid. Confirm, or lower it to about 0.4.
10. **`.txm-h2` at weight 300 on dark sections was not checked against the mobile sizes** (R-06), as the plan asked. Light display weights can look thin at 30px on low-density phones.
11. **The hero eyebrow wraps to two uppercase lines at 390px** (R-06). Wide-tracked uppercase over two lines reads heavier than intended; a shorter mobile eyebrow or less tracking under 640px would keep it to one line.
12. **Every icon on the site comes from one stock library** (antislop-ui, "Lucide icons"). Existing choice, not introduced in this pass; listed for completeness.
